# Links

Your email and social links. One file: `links.md`.

They appear in the footer of every page and in the Contact section on the home and about pages.

## Add a link

Copy one block (all three lines) and change the values. Keep the two spaces at the start of each line:

```yaml
  - platform: "GitHub"
    handle: "@yourname"
    url: "https://github.com/yourname"
```

- `platform`: the name shown (Instagram, X, GitHub, LinkedIn, YouTube…).
- `handle`: optional. What's shown as the link text. Without it, the URL is shown.
- `url`: must start with `https://`.

Links show in the order you list them.

## Remove a link

Delete its three lines.

## Change your email

Edit the `email:` line.

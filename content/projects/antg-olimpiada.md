A platform for preparing students for the national math olympiad with practice problems, blogs, and free/paid exams. Me and my olympiad teacher decided to build [ANTG Olimpiada](https://www.antgolimpiada.com) because in Azerbaijani language, no such thing has ever been built.

I started building the website with **Claude Code** right away on _August 18, 2026_.

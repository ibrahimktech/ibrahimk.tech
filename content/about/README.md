# About

Who you are. One file: `about.md`.

## The settings at the top (between the `---` lines)

| Field         | What it does                                                    |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `name`        | Your full name. Big heading on the home page, in page titles.   |
| `brand`       | Short name in the top-left corner and browser tabs ("Ibrahim K"). |
| `role`        | Line under your name on the home page.                          |
| `location`    | Shown on the home page and in the footer.                       |
| `intro`       | The paragraph on the home page.                                 |
| `description` | One sentence for Google results and link previews.              |

Keep the quotes around each value.

## The text below the settings

That is the `/about` page. Write it like a normal document:

- Empty line between paragraphs.
- `## Heading` for a section (like "What I'm working on").
- `- item` for a bullet list.

Your email and social links are added at the bottom automatically from `content/links/links.md`.
